Qualifications

A step that matches a condition requires its quals before sign-off. A condition's When can join several fields with AND or OR, in groups, for example Type is VT AND Weld Color is Straw. Joint Details fields are checked as soon as a step with a Qualification Check opens. Sign-off fields are read from the step being signed, once they're filled in.

Controlled Material is Yes when MCL 1 or MCL 2 requires traceability in Admin > Material Traceability. Titanium is Yes when either Material Type is marked Titanium in Admin > Material Classification.
